fig. 01 — archive memory

she left without a word.

the silence that followed wasn't empty; it was heavy. a dense frequency that settled into the concrete walls of the apartment. i found a half-drilled cup of coffee. a receipt from the corner store. fragments of a sudden departure.

"there was no argument. no final glance back. just the sharp click of the deadbolt locking from the outside."

every object left behind became a forensic artifact. the alignment of shoes by the door. the specific way the blanket was folded. i cataloged everything, searching for the reason why. but the only truth was the absence.
